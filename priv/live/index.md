## We Are Looking for Challenging Projects

As veterans of the tech industry, hands–on engineers, we’re working to **de–risk software

shipping exactly what they need on a _predictable timeline_ at a _fair price_.

Being a small studio is a strategic advantage. We are project managers, solutions architects,
and hands–on engineers all at once, doing **_high–impact, low–overhead, bespoke development_**.

**We focus on quality over quantity**, only working with a few clients at time, so your project

### FAQ: _What are your rates?_

<aside class="right">Scope changes are encouraged, not negotiated.</aside>

Between **_$5,000 and $15,000 per week_**, depending on the pace of the project, **paid up–front
each Monday**.

We roadmap the project, set milestones at **a pace we know we can sustain**, so you never have
to stress about missing deadlines or minimum contract lengths.

**_You do_**. You’ve payed for it, it belongs to you. We **don’t do lock–ins** and we **never
claim any code or data** we produce for your project.
