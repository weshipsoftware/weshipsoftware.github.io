Release Notes:
Cumberland Farms/SmartPay

We helped a business with millions of messages a day distributed over tens of thousands of IoT devices build a data pipeline that ingested, stored, and analyzed every single message in real-time.

Our client had tens of thousands of EV chargers that would send upwards of tens of thousands of messages a day each. For those not keeping track, 10,000 * 10,000 = 100,000,000 messages minimum, AKA an overwhelming amount of messages. They wanted to migrate these EV chargers to an existing management platform as a new system of record. They also wanted real-time status updates available for each EV charger, of course, why not? We worked with them to understand their internal infrastructure and business limitations to architect a data pipeline that worked within their existing infrastructure ecosystem. It was too labor-intensive for their employees to onboard each charger. Can you imagine a single person manually adding +20,000 EV chargers via a form? We could, thought it was hilarious, but took pity on the poor soul so we built in automatic EV charger registration, no human interaction needed. Our client had their own customers using these chargers, and due to the significance of their business operations, this system had to be secure, reliable, and durable. We chose an established open standard between distributed systems to ensure reliability, security, and structured communication. Durability was added by leveraging message tracking, automatic retries, preventing duplicate messages being recorded more than once, and a self-healing system capable of restarting itself and resuming on failure. The outcome was a secure, reliable, and durable data pipeline with 99.9% uptime.
