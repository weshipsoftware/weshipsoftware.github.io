InCharge Energy

Richard helped InCharge Energy ingest, store, and analyze millions of daily messages in real–time.

InCharge Energy is a full–lifecycle energy infrastructure partner delivering EV charging, electrical, and distributed energy solutions—from design and construction through long–term operations and maintenance.

10,000 * 10,000 = 100,000,000 messages/day… minimum.

One of InCharge Energy’s biggest customers needed to onboard tens of thousands of EV chargers that would each send tens of thousands of messages per day through InCharge Energy’s asset management platform.

The Challenge

  1. Reliably scale to handle the growing demand.
  2. Connect new chargers without manually onboarding one–by–one.
  3. Provide accurate, real–time insights for customers.

The Solution

From 2024 through 2026, Richard worked at InCharge Energy building and scaling a data pipeline that now processes hundreds of millions of messages per day in real–time. His work was foundational for ingesting, storing, and segmenting the massive amounts of data needed to provide the live status insights that customers demand.

Open standards don’t require onboarding.

Richard researched and chose an open standard for communicating between distributed systems, providing security and reliability through structured messaging. He managed to reach 99.9+% uptime by designing the system to self–heal by restarting itself after upstream issues, picking up where it left off, while handling automatic retries and idempotency to make sure it never misses a message.

The Result

The pipeline is now secure, reliable, and durable at scale, allowing InCharge Energy to quickly add any number of EV chargers and immediately provide valuable insights for their customers.
